How it works

Bayvs normalvs boss Red (left)2×1× Black (middle)1×2× Yellow (right)1.5×1.5×
  • Your Mate rank goes Junior → Senior → Veteran → Elite → Master. Each all clear promotes you one step, and higher ranks pull better sidekicks more often.
  • The first three deploys fill Red, Black, Yellow. After that a deploy only helps if it beats your lowest rank, and it replaces that one (ties: Red, then Yellow, then Black). Equal or lower is wasted.
  • Sidekicks fire straight up into a shared HP pool: Red pours into the mobs, Black into the bosses, Yellow into the mobs and then the bosses.
  • Levels cost 100 + 5 per level, up to 10. A level keeps the value of the rank it was bought at, so levels on an S are worth the most.
  • Each wave drops in all at once, from 12 enemies in wave 1 to 30 in wave 10. Waves 1–2 are mobs only. Waves 3–5 bring one boss, 6–8 two, and 9–10 three, with a bigger final boss in wave 10. Mobs get tougher every wave.
  • Wave 1 lasts 1:30, every wave after that 1:00. Leftover enemies carry into the next wave. Reach 50 and the deployment ends. Clear every enemy in wave 10 for an all clear.
