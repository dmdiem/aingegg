Each combo was run through this game's battle sim. It assumes you level highest rank first once you stop pressing Go, and that a better roll replaces your weakest bay. Advice aims for the all clear. Manual mode is for planning a real Maple run.

How to play

  • Go deploys a random aingegg (E to S). Your first three fill Red, Black and Yellow. After that, a better roll replaces your weakest (a random one if they tie).
  • Collect Aing points every minute and spend them on Go or on levels. Tap an aingegg, then Auto-level to level it to 10 for you.
  • Red is strong vs mobs, Black vs bosses, Yellow vs both.
  • Don't let 50 cows pile up. Clear wave 10 to win.
  • All clear to unlock the next Mate rank. Use Rank Override to play at any rank you've unlocked.
